## Rule

In the philoserf/notes Obsidian vault, 3–7 paragraph paragraph-form explorations of a single idea belong in `Drafts/`, not `Reference/`. Third-person voice does NOT make them Reference; the published-notes corpus shows the same shape, often without first-person "I."

## Why

The user's published essay form is exactly this shape. See `Published/Adulthood as Latent Capacity.md`, `Published/Cost of Knowing.md`, `Published/Cassandra Inversion.md` — all third-person or sparing first-person, all paragraph-form, all 3–7 paragraphs. Reference is for material consulted but not written by the vault owner: external clippings, factual tables, taxonomies, lists, attributed sources.

Routing the user's own developed thinking to Reference buries it where it can't progress through the publishing pipeline (`draft` → `develop` → `review` → `publish`).

## How to Apply

When triaging inbox notes for routing, ask first: is this a paragraph-form exploration of a single idea?

- **Yes** → Drafts
- **List, taxonomy, table, sectioned bullet reference, attributed external source** → Reference
- **Thin/incubating/uncertain shape** → Holding
- **Confidential/private** → Private
- **RPG content** → Traveller

The destinations.md "first-person voice" signal for Drafts is necessary but the user's drafts often lack it — paragraph-form exploration of meaning is the stronger signal.
